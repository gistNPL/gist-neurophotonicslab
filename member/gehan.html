Research

Gehan Fatima is a REMAP InnoCORE Research Fellow at GIST. She earned an integrated MS–PhD degree at KAIST, where she investigated molecular mechanisms that regulate lymphatic vascular development and homeostasis under the supervision of Prof. Gou Young Koh.

Her research focuses on neurovascular and lymphatic dysfunction in neurological diseases, particularly Alzheimer’s disease. She combines vascular biology, advanced optical imaging, artificial intelligence, and quantitative behavioral analysis to study changes associated with disease.

She develops computational methods for behavioral phenotyping in mouse models of Alzheimer’s disease using DeepLabCut, Motion Sequencing (MoSeq), UMAP, and AI-based image segmentation. She also studies how remodeling of peripheral and meningeal lymphatics may relate to neurodegeneration and is investigating the potential effects of ultra-high-frequency magnetic stimulation on meningeal lymphatic function.
